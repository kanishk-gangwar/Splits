## Percentages keep their meaning, and names stay your own

**Switching a split to Percent no longer reads your amounts as percentages.** Pinning ₹300 on a
₹900 expense and then tapping Percent used to show a literal `300`, which the app took as 300%
of the bill. It now says `33.33` — the same slice you had already chosen. Going the other way
works too: 25% on a ₹900 expense becomes ₹225.

Rows you never typed into are untouched by this. They stay automatic and keep showing their
computed share as a greyed placeholder, whichever mode you are in.

### Who can change what

- **Only you can edit your own name.** The rename button now appears on your row and nowhere
  else. A name is how you are addressed all over the group, so nobody else gets to retype it —
  the admin included.
- **Only the admin can remove a participant.** Everyone else no longer sees the button. The
  admin still cannot remove themselves, because a group with no admin has nobody left who can
  delete it.

Both buttons are hidden rather than greyed out, and non-admins get a line saying why.

One known gap: a participant who has not joined yet has nobody who can fix a typo in their
name. Remove and re-add them if that happens — which works right up until they appear in an
expense.

### Nothing else changed

No data migration, no server change. Existing groups, expenses and balances are exactly as you
left them.
