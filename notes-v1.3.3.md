## The admin can fix names again

1.3.2 locked renaming down to your own row and nobody else's — including the admin's. That went
one step too far: the admin types the participant list, so a typo in somebody who has not joined
yet had nobody left who could correct it.

**The admin can now rename anyone.** Everyone else can still rename themselves and only
themselves, which was the point of the change. Removing a participant is unchanged: admin only,
and the admin still cannot remove their own row.

Everything else from 1.3.2 stands, including percent splits keeping their meaning when you
switch modes.
