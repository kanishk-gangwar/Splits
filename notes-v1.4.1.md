## Settle up without scrolling

The list of payments that clears a group sat underneath every member's balance. In a group of
eight that meant scrolling past eight cards to reach the one thing you actually came to act
on — and it got worse the bigger the group, which is exactly when settling up matters most.

**Settle up is its own tab now.** The payments are the first thing on it, and the tab label
carries the count — `Settle up 7` — so you can see there is something outstanding without
opening it. The Balances tab keeps only the per-person balances and ends there.

## The People tab is gone, and nothing went with it

That tab listed who had joined the group. Group settings already listed exactly the same
thing, with more besides — add a participant, rename, remove, share the invite. Two copies of
one list can only drift apart, so the duplicate is the one that went.

Everything it did is still reachable. Picking your name is still the prompt at the top of the
group when you have not claimed one, and the invite button is still in the group's title bar.

**Group settings now splits participants under *Joined* and *Not joined yet*,** each with its
own count, rather than making you read the second line of every row to work out who is still
missing. That second line is now used only for what a heading cannot say — which participant
is the admin.

## Also

Your share, what you paid, and the fewest-payments settle-up from 1.4.0 are unchanged; this
release is about where they live and how quickly you get to them.
