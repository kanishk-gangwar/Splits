## Insights starts with you, and stays short

The first version of Insights listed every member and every expense for the month in one
scroll. On a busy group that is a long page of other people's dinners before you reach your
own numbers — and once you get there, your list is just as long as theirs.

**You are at the top.** The header now shows the group total plus *your* share and what you
paid. Your row sits under a You section, highlighted, even if somebody else spent more that
month. Everyone else is underneath, biggest spend first.

**Expenses stay folded until you ask.** Each person shows how much they spent and how many
expenses they were in. Tap a row to open their list — only one person at a time, so the
screen does not grow without bound. Your row starts open. If someone has more than four
expenses, you see the first four and a button for the rest.

A thin bar on each row is that person's slice of the month, so you can compare without
opening anything.

## Also

Expense-list paging from 1.6.0 is unchanged: three calendar months at a time, then the next
three when you scroll. This build needs no Supabase changes.
