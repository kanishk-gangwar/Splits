Splits

Split expenses with friends on Android and iOS. No accounts, no phone numbers — share a link and everyone picks their own name.

Share a link, pick your name

Opening an invite shows the group's participants. Tap whichever one is you.

Settlements aren't spending

Paying someone back updates the balance but stays out of the group total.

Archive, hide, or delete

Only the admin can delete a group. Anyone can hide it from their own device.

Works offline

Everything is local first and reconciles with the server when you pull to refresh.
