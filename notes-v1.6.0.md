## The expenses list no longer loads the whole history at once

A group that has been around for a year was painting every expense the moment you opened it.
That is a lot of cards to compose for a list you were only going to scroll through the last
few weeks of.

**The expenses tab now shows three calendar months at a time.** Open a group in September and
you see July, August and September. Scroll to the bottom and a loader appears; a moment later
the next three months join the list. Balances and settle-up still use the full history, because
those numbers are wrong if a year-old dinner is missing.

## Insights, from the three dots

The three dots in the group header used to jump straight into settings. They now open a menu:
Insights, and Group settings.

**Insights is a month-by-month picture of who spent what.** The total for that month sits at
the top. Every member is listed underneath with their share, what they fronted, and the
expenses they were part of. Settlements stay out of it — they move money around, they are not
new spending.

The month and year are both changeable: arrows step one month at a time, and tapping the
label opens a picker so you can jump. You cannot walk into the future, and you cannot walk
past the group's first expense.

## Also

Nothing else changed. Settlements still land once, the admin can still free a name, and this
build needs no Supabase changes.
