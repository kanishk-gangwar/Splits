## Several people paid? Now you can say so

When a bill is paid by more than one person, tap **More than one person paid** under "Paid by",
tick everyone who chipped in, and choose:

- **Equally** — the amount is shared evenly between the payers.
- **Exact** — type what each person paid. Anyone you leave blank covers an even part of
  what is left.

Balances, "You paid", and settle-up all count each payer's real contribution. The expense list
shows "2 people paid ₹3,000", and the details page lists who paid what.

**Everyone in the group should update.** Older versions only understand one payer: they show
the biggest contributor as the person who paid it all, and editing such an entry from an older
version turns it back into a single-payer entry.


## Splits on iPhone, in the browser

iPhone users can now use Splits without the App Store. Open
**https://kanishk-gangwar.github.io/Splits/app/** in Safari, tap **Share → Add to Home Screen**,
and it opens full screen like any other app (iOS 18.2 or newer). Invite links get an
"Open in the web app" button on iPhone. Groups are kept on that phone and sync with everyone
else, exactly like the Android app.
